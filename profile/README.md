This is **PaddleHQ-Forks** — the home for our forks of public open-source projects. Looking for first-party Paddle code, SDKs, or our OpenAPI spec? You probably want [PaddleHQ](https://github.com/PaddleHQ) instead.

## Why this org exists

We host our forks of third-party OSS here, separate from our first-party code in [PaddleHQ](https://github.com/PaddleHQ). That lets us:

- Keep upstream conventions intact, so our changes are easy to land back upstream.
- Avoid forks ambiently inheriting `PaddleHQ` organisation secrets.
- Keep our security tooling (action-pinning policies, etc.) focused on first-party code.

## What lives here

Each repo here is a **tracking fork** of an upstream public OSS project — the fork relationship points back to the upstream repo on GitHub, and our intent is to send our changes home.

If we've effectively adopted a project (upstream is dormant, or our version has diverged so far that we have no realistic plan to merge back), it lives in [PaddleHQ](https://github.com/PaddleHQ) instead and is brought up to our first-party standards. This org is *only* for forks we still intend to contribute back to.

## We 💛 open source

Like most companies, we use open source software every day at Paddle. Open source helps us ship great products for our customers, as well as making our working lives more enjoyable. We're grateful to the open source community, and this org is one of the ways we try to give back — by staying close to upstream and pushing our changes home rather than hoarding them in a private patch.
